      Your own words

      Add your own cards to any category — inside jokes, names, anything. Saved on this device and shuffled into the deck.

      How to play

      1. New game → name your teams and pick a target (20 / 30 / 40).
      2. On your turn, spin for a random category, then the describer gets word after word for 30 seconds.
      3. Describe it without saying the word, any part of it, or what it rhymes with. Right? Got it. Stuck? Pass (once, and you can go back to it). Broke a rule? Foul to bin the card.
      4. Each Got it is a point; scores track automatically. First team to the target reaches the finish.
      5. To win you must take the all-play: pick one of two cards and describe it with no timer — your team has to guess before anyone else shouts it, or you try again next turn.
